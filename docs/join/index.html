Join the lab

Interested in joining the Alcalde Lab?

If you’re curious about what microbes do in soils and around plant roots (or frankly, just what microbes do), we’d like to hear from you.

We welcome inquiries from people with a wide range of backgrounds, including those looking for their first research experience. We value curiosity, motivation, and a willingness to learn across disciplines and work collaboratively. In the lab, you’ll have opportunities to contribute to ongoing research, develop questions of your own, and build skills for the career you want to pursue.

Prospective students, postdoctoral researchers, and collaborators may contact Rei at rea5465@psu.edu.
